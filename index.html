When Daniel joined a small design company, he expected his new colleagues to be competitive and rather distant. On his first day, however, he discovered that the team was much more cooperative than he had expected.

Before Daniel had arrived, the team had already agreed on a new way of sharing tasks. Everyone had a clear responsibility, but colleagues were encouraged to help each other when deadlines became difficult.

Daniel was initially quite shy, so he didn't ask many questions. After a few weeks, he became more confident. He realised that his colleagues were reliable and easy-going, and he started to enjoy working with them.

By the end of his first month, Daniel had completed several projects. He had also learned that his first impression had been wrong: the team wasn't competitive at all. Their different personalities helped them work well together.
